## 一句话

研究系统的目的，是让你的判断可追溯、可修正，而不是让你知道得更多。

## 信息分层

- **一手**：财报、招股书、监管文件、公司公告、链上数据。成本高，可信度最高。
- **二手**：书籍、研报、深度长文。已经被加工过，带着作者的立场。
- **三手**：短视频、群聊、财经媒体快讯。作用是提示，不是依据。

大部分人的问题是把三手当一手用。一个简单的纪律：看到任何结论，先找它的原始出处，找不到就不采信。

## 笔记要分三层

- **事实**：可以核对的数字与事件。写清来源与日期。
- **判断**：你对事实的解释。必须写明"如果看到什么，我就错了"。
- **待验证**：还不确定的假设。定期回看，该删的删。

三层混在一起，笔记就会变成自我说服的工具。分开写，你才能看到自己哪一层最薄弱。

## 决策日志

每次买入前写四行：买入理由、预期收益来源、证伪条件、复盘日期。

复盘时只问一件事：当初的理由现在还成立吗？不成立就卖，哪怕正在赚钱。成立就拿着，哪怕正在亏钱。这是把情绪从决策里剔除的唯一办法。

## 节奏

- **每季度**：看财报，确认关键指标是否偏离。
- **每年**：检查持仓逻辑是否变化，做一次再平衡。
- **每三年**：检查自己的能力圈是否扩大，方法是否需要更新。

频率太高会过度交易，太低会错过逻辑的破裂点。

## 年度检查清单

1. 我的资产配置比例是否还在目标区间？
2. 每个持仓的买入理由是否仍然成立？
3. 这一年我的收益里，多少来自市场，多少来自判断？
4. 有没有哪笔交易是完全凭情绪做的？
5. 我今年新学的东西，有没有改变任何一个决策？
6. 费用与税，是否还在可接受的水平？
7. 如果明天开始市场关闭五年，我的组合能不能撑住？

## 最后

这份手册给的是判断框架，不是答案。框架的价值在于，当有人向你承诺高收益时，你有六个问题可以问回去。

投资是少数"努力不一定有回报，但不努力一定被收割"的领域。把精力放在能积累的地方：读财报的能力、识别骗局的能力、控制情绪的能力。这三样谁也拿不走。

## In one sentence

A research system exists so your judgment can be traced and corrected, not so you can know more things.

## Layering information

- **Primary.** Statements, prospectuses, regulatory filings, company announcements, on-chain data. Expensive, most reliable.
- **Secondary.** Books, research notes, long-form analysis. Already processed, carrying someone's position.
- **Tertiary.** Short video, group chats, financial media alerts. A prompt, not a basis.

Most people treat tertiary as primary. One rule fixes a lot: for any conclusion, find its original source. If you cannot, do not adopt it.

## Notes in three layers

- **Fact.** Numbers and events you can verify. Record the source and the date.
- **Judgment.** Your interpretation. It must include the condition that would prove you wrong.
- **Open question.** Assumptions still unresolved. Review on schedule and delete the dead ones.

Mixed together, notes become a tool of self-persuasion. Kept apart, you can see which layer is weakest.

## Decision log

Before every purchase, four lines: why, where the return comes from, what would falsify it, when to review.

At review, one question only. Does the original reason still hold? If not, sell, even at a profit. If yes, hold, even at a loss. That is the only reliable way to take emotion out of the decision.

## Cadence

- **Quarterly.** Read the statements, check whether key metrics drifted.
- **Annually.** Re-examine each thesis, rebalance once.
- **Every three years.** Ask whether your circle of competence grew and whether the method needs replacing.

Too frequent and you overtrade. Too rare and you miss the point where the logic broke.

## Annual checklist

1. Is the allocation still inside the target band?
2. Does every holding still satisfy its original reason?
3. How much of this year's return came from the market, how much from judgment?
4. Was any trade made purely on emotion?
5. Did anything you learned this year change a decision?
6. Are fees and taxes still acceptable?
7. If the market closed for five years tomorrow, would the portfolio survive?

## Closing

This handbook offers a framework, not answers. Its value shows up when someone promises you a high return and you have six questions to send back.

Investing is one of the few fields where effort does not guarantee a return, but its absence guarantees being harvested. Put effort where it accumulates: reading statements, spotting fraud, controlling yourself. Nobody can take those three away.
