## 一句话

决定你账户波动的，是股债比例，不是你选了哪只基金。定投是行为工具，不是收益机器。

## 先定比例，再挑产品

配置比例应该从一个问题倒推：市场跌一半，你还能睡着吗？

能承受两成回撤，股票比例就别超过四成。这不是收益最优解，是你能坚持十年的解。一个坚持不住的完美组合，收益是零。

常见的锚点：三成到五成股票适合刚开始的人，六成到八成适合经历过两轮完整周期的人。年龄公式（一百减年龄）粗糙但方向对。

## 再平衡是唯一免费的高抛低吸

设定阈值，比如股债偏离目标五个百分点就调回。它的价值在纪律，不在预测。牛市里它会逼你卖一点，熊市里它会逼你买一点，正好和情绪相反。

一年一次，或者偏离超阈值时做一次。做太频繁只是给券商交手续费。

## 定投的真实数学

定投在单边下跌的市场里优于一次性投入，在长期上涨的市场里劣于一次性投入。长期看，大部分市场大部分时间是上涨的，所以定投的期望收益略低于一次性。

那为什么还推荐定投？因为它解决了普通人最难的问题：工资是月月到账的现金流，而择时需要判断力。定投把择时问题变成纪律问题。

三种情况应该一次性：手里已有一大笔钱且标的明确低估、有明确的期限匹配、心理上能承受短期浮亏。

## 三个定投幻觉

- **定投一定赚**。标的选错，定投只是延缓亏损。定投降低的是择时风险，不是标的风险。
- **定投越久越好**。定投的积累期有限，长期只投不调整，后期会变成一次性投入的变体。
- **止盈止损是必须的**。对宽基指数，频繁止盈往往错过后面的涨幅。真正需要止盈的是估值极端时。

## 别忽略三个隐性成本

- **费率**。指数基金的管理费与托管费，长期差一个点很致命。
- **汇率**。投海外资产，汇率波动可能吃掉大半收益，也可能白送。
- **税**。股息税、资本利得税、跨境投资的预扣税，各国不同，先查清再动手。

## 自检

你的组合里，每一块资产的存在理由能否用一句话说清？说不清的那块，通常是看了某篇文章加的。

## In one sentence

Your drawdown is set by your stock-bond split, not by which fund you picked. Regular investing is a behaviour tool, not a return engine.

## Set the ratio before picking products

Work backwards from one question. If the market halves, can you still sleep?

If you can tolerate a twenty percent drawdown, keep equities at or below forty percent. That is not the return-maximising answer, it is the one you can hold for ten years. A perfect portfolio you abandon has a return of zero.

Rough anchors: thirty to fifty percent equities for people starting out, sixty to eighty for people who have lived through two full cycles. The age formula (one hundred minus age) is crude but points the right way.

## Rebalancing is the only free buy-low-sell-high

Set a band. When the equity share drifts five points from target, bring it back. The value is discipline, not prediction. It forces selling into strength and buying into weakness, the opposite of instinct.

Once a year, or when the band breaks. More often than that just pays the broker.

## The arithmetic of regular investing

Dollar-cost averaging beats lump sum in a market that falls monotonically, and loses to lump sum in one that rises over time. Most markets rise most of the time, so the expected return of averaging is slightly lower.

So why recommend it? Because it solves the problem retail investors actually have. Salary arrives monthly; timing requires judgment. Averaging converts a timing problem into a discipline problem.

Lump sum makes sense in three cases: you already hold a large sum, the asset is clearly undervalued, and you can sit through an immediate drawdown.

## Three illusions

- **Averaging always wins.** Pick the wrong asset and averaging only delays the loss. It reduces timing risk, not asset risk.
- **Longer is always better.** The accumulation phase is finite. Investing without ever adjusting turns into a lump sum by another name.
- **Stop-loss and take-profit are mandatory.** On broad indices, frequent profit-taking usually misses the subsequent run. What deserves trimming is extreme valuation.

## Three costs people forget

- **Fees.** A one point difference in management and custody fees is lethal over decades.
- **Currency.** On overseas assets, FX can eat most of the return, or hand you a bonus.
- **Tax.** Dividend tax, capital gains tax, withholding on cross-border holdings. Check before you buy, not after.
