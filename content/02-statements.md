## 一句话

财报不是用来算精确价值的，是用来排除公司的。看懂它，你能在一小时内否掉九成标的。

## 三张表的关系

**利润表**讲一段时间里赚了多少，**资产负债表**讲某一天拥有什么、欠了什么，**现金流量表**讲钱实际怎么进出。三张表必须能互相对上，对不上就是有故事。

最该盯的一行是**经营活动现金流**。净利润增长而经营现金流不增长，通常意味着利润停在应收账款和存货里，没变成钱。

**自由现金流 = 经营现金流 − 资本开支**。这是股东真正能拿走的部分，也是估值的地基。一家公司常年自由现金流为负还要不断融资，商业模式就有问题。

## 用杜邦分解看穿高 ROE

净资产收益率 = 净利率 × 总资产周转率 × 权益乘数。

高 ROE 可能来自三个完全不同的地方：产品有定价权（净利率高）、生意转得快（周转高）、借了很多钱（杠杆高）。前两个是好生意，第三个是风险。只看 ROE 数字不看来源，等于把杠杆当能力。

## 六个红旗

- 应收账款增速长期高于收入增速。
- 存货周转天数持续变长，尤其在有保质期或技术迭代快的行业。
- 商誉占净资产比例过高，并且连续计提减值。
- 经营现金流与净利润长期背离。
- 非经常性损益占利润比重高，比如卖房、卖股权、政府补贴。
- 审计意见不是标准无保留，或者频繁更换审计机构。

## 财报的边界

财报是后视镜，反映的是过去。它不能告诉你行业正在发生什么变化，也不能告诉你管理层是否诚实，只能告诉你有没有可疑的地方。

会计政策有合法选择空间，同样的业务可以做出不同的利润。跨公司比较时，先看会计政策是否一致，再看数字。

## 常见错误

- 只看净利润和市盈率，不看现金流。
- 把一次性收益当成经营能力。
- 用一期的数据下结论，不看五年趋势。
- 相信"调整后利润"这类公司自己发明的指标。

## 自检

拿到一家公司，能否在二十分钟内回答：钱从哪来、赚的钱去哪了、有没有靠借钱撑增长？

## In one sentence

Financial statements are not for computing precise value. They are for disqualifying companies. Read them well and you can reject ninety percent of candidates within an hour.

## How the three statements connect

The income statement covers a period. The balance sheet is a snapshot of a day. The cash flow statement shows what actually moved. The three must reconcile. When they do not, something is being explained away.

Watch **operating cash flow** first. Profit growing while operating cash flow stalls usually means earnings are sitting in receivables and inventory rather than turning into money.

**Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capital expenditure.** That is what owners can actually take out, and it is the foundation of any valuation. A company with chronically negative free cash flow that keeps raising money has a business model problem, not a timing problem.

## DuPont, to see through a high ROE

Return on equity = net margin × asset turnover × equity multiplier.

A high ROE can come from three unrelated places: pricing power (high margin), a fast business (high turnover), or borrowed money (high leverage). The first two are good businesses. The third is risk. Quoting ROE without its source mistakes leverage for skill.

## Six red flags

- Receivables growing faster than revenue for several periods.
- Inventory days rising, especially where products expire or technology turns over fast.
- Goodwill large relative to equity, with repeated impairments.
- Operating cash flow diverging from net income.
- A large share of profit from non-recurring items: asset sales, equity disposals, subsidies.
- A qualified audit opinion, or frequent auditor changes.

## Limits

Statements look backwards. They cannot tell you how the industry is changing or whether management is honest. They only tell you where to look.

Accounting policy leaves legitimate room to move profit. Before comparing two companies, check whether the policies are comparable.

## Common mistakes

- Reading profit and P/E while ignoring cash flow.
- Treating a one-off gain as operating skill.
- Drawing conclusions from a single period instead of a five-year trend.
- Trusting company-defined metrics such as "adjusted profit".

## Self-check

Given a company, can you answer in twenty minutes: where the money comes from, where the profit went, and whether growth is funded by debt?
