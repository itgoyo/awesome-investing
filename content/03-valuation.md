## 一句话

估值不是算出精确数字，是判断"这个价格买入，最坏情况我亏多少"。安全边际是折扣，不是小数点。

## 四把尺子，各有适用场景

- **市盈率 PE**：适合盈利稳定的公司。周期股在利润高点时 PE 最低，最危险。
- **市净率 PB**：适合银行、保险这类资产说话的行业。轻资产公司用它没有意义。
- **EV/EBITDA**：适合重资产、高负债，把资本结构差异拉平再比。
- **市销率 PS**：只在亏损但高增长的阶段用，且必须配合毛利率判断。

**现金流折现 DCF** 理论上最严谨，实际上对假设极度敏感。折现率动一个点，结果能差三成。它的正确用法是给出一个区间，以及做敏感性分析看哪个假设最关键，而不是输出一个"内在价值 87.3 元"。

## 安全边际怎么做

先估一个保守的价值区间，只在市场价格明显低于区间下沿时买入。这个折价不是为了多赚，是为了给判断错误留出空间。

折价要多少，取决于生意有多确定。稳定收租的资产折价两成足够，技术路线还没定的行业折价五成也未必够。

## 能力圈不是借口

说"我看不懂"很诚实，但不能对所有行业都说。能力圈是一个需要主动扩建的东西，路径通常是：先做一个行业，读五年财报，跟踪三家公司的季度数据，形成自己的判断框架，再横向复制。

真正的边界不是行业，是可预测性。十年后这家公司还在卖同样的东西吗？如果答案模糊，就别用 DCF，改用更保守的资产法或直接放弃。

## 三个估值幻觉

- **便宜不等于好**。低 PE 可能是在反映生意正在恶化，市场提前定价了。
- **高增长可以合理化任何价格**。用 PEG 把四十倍 PE 说成合理，是牛市里最常见的自我说服。
- **历史估值区间可靠**。行业结构变了，历史分位就没有参考价值。

## 自检

你买入的理由，能否用三句话说清，并且其中不包含"别人都在买"和"应该会涨"？

## In one sentence

Valuation is not a precise number. It is an answer to "if I buy here, how much do I lose in the bad case". Margin of safety is a discount, not a decimal place.

## Four rulers, four jobs

- **P/E** suits stable earnings. For cyclicals the lowest P/E often marks the peak of the cycle, which is exactly when it looks cheapest.
- **P/B** suits banks and insurers, where assets carry the business. It says nothing about asset-light companies.
- **EV/EBITDA** suits capital-intensive, leveraged businesses. It neutralises capital structure before comparing.
- **P/S** only fits loss-making growth, and only alongside gross margin.

**DCF** is the most rigorous in theory and the most sensitive in practice. Move the discount rate one point and the output moves thirty percent. Use it to produce a range and a sensitivity table that shows which assumption dominates, not a single "intrinsic value of 87.3".

## How to set the discount

Estimate a conservative range first, then buy only when the market is clearly below the low end. The discount is not for extra return, it is for being wrong.

How deep the discount must be depends on certainty. For a stable rent-collecting asset, twenty percent is plenty. For an industry whose technology is unsettled, fifty percent may not be enough.

## Circle of competence is not an excuse

Saying "I do not understand this" is honest. Saying it about every industry is avoidance. A circle of competence is built, not discovered. The usual path: pick one industry, read five years of statements, track three companies quarterly, build your own framework, then copy the method sideways.

The real boundary is not the sector, it is predictability. Will this company still sell the same thing in ten years? If that is fuzzy, skip DCF and use a conservative asset-based approach, or skip the company.

## Three valuation illusions

- **Cheap is not good.** A low multiple can be the market pricing in deterioration ahead of the reports.
- **Growth justifies any price.** Using PEG to bless forty times earnings is the most common self-persuasion in a bull market.
- **Historical ranges are reliable.** Once industry structure changes, the percentile table is decoration.

## Self-check

Can you state your reason for buying in three sentences, without the words "everyone is buying" or "it should go up"?
