## 一句话

长期收益来自企业赚的钱，不来自别人接盘的价格。想清楚这一点，后面所有动作才有意义。

## 收益的三个来源

股票的长期回报只有一个公式：

**总回报 = 盈利增长 + 股息 + 估值变化**

前两项由企业经营决定，第三项由市场情绪决定。十年以上的维度，估值变化会被均值回归抹平，收益回落到盈利与股息。这就是为什么预测明年涨跌没什么用，而判断一家公司十年后还在不在赚钱非常有用。

债券和存款的收益来自利息，条款写死，天花板也写死。它们的作用不是赚钱，是让你在股票跌一半的时候不用卖股票。

黄金、收藏品、加密货币不产生现金流，价格完全由下一个买家决定。这类资产可以有配置价值，但它们的收益不来自"经营"，判断依据完全不同，别用同一套估值方法。

## 你只能控制三件事

- **买入价格**。同一家公司，二十倍市盈率买入和四十倍买入，十年后的年化收益差一大截。
- **持有时间**。复利需要时间做乘方，中途下车就没了。
- **成本**。管理费、交易佣金、税、买卖价差，这些是确定的负数。

市场给多少倍估值，你控制不了。所以精力应该放在这三件事上，而不是猜下个季度的GDP。

## 指数是默认选项，不是懒人的选择

买下整个市场，等于放弃了选股的胜负，也放弃了选股的成本。费率差一个百分点，三十年下来终值差两成以上。这个差距不需要任何判断力就能拿到，这是普通人最大的优势。

主动管理不是不能赢，是要在扣费之后赢。A股散户交易占比高、定价效率低于美股，超额收益的空间确实更大，但这不等于你能拿到。判断标准很朴素：连续五年、扣费之后、相对同一基准，还赢。

## 别把 beta 当 alpha

牛市里人人都是股神。账户涨了百分之三十，先问一句：同期指数涨了多少？超额部分才是你的判断，剩下的是市场给的。

杠杆会把 beta 放大，也会把回撤放大。用杠杆赚到的钱，在市场转向时会被同一个杠杆拿走。这不是运气问题，是数学问题。

## 三个自检问题

1. 你今年赚的钱，是公司赚的，还是别人亏的？
2. 如果明天开始市场关闭五年，你还敢持有现在这些标的吗？
3. 你的收益里，有多少能归因到自己的判断，多少归因到市场？

答不上来，就还在赌，不在投资。

## In one sentence

Long-run returns come from what businesses earn, not from what the next buyer pays. Every decision downstream depends on accepting this.

## Where return comes from

For equities there is one formula:

**Total return = earnings growth + dividends + change in valuation**

The first two are set by the business. The third is set by mood. Over ten years or more, valuation change mean-reverts and the return falls back to earnings and dividends. That is why forecasting next year's index level is close to useless, and judging whether a company still earns money in ten years is not.

Bonds and deposits pay a contractual interest rate. Their ceiling is fixed. Their job is not to make you rich, it is to keep you from selling equities when they halve.

Gold, collectibles and crypto produce no cash flow. Price is whatever the next buyer accepts. They can deserve a place in a portfolio, but their return does not come from operations, so the valuation toolkit is different. Do not apply an equity framework to them.

## Three things you control

- **Entry price.** The same business bought at twenty times earnings and at forty times produces very different decade-long returns.
- **Holding period.** Compounding needs time as an exponent. Selling early removes it.
- **Cost.** Management fees, commissions, taxes and spreads are certain negatives.

Nobody controls the multiple the market will pay. Spend the effort on the three items above instead of guessing next quarter's GDP.

## The index is the default, not the lazy option

Buying the whole market gives up the chance to pick winners and gives up the cost of picking. One percentage point of fee difference compounds into a terminal value difference above twenty percent over thirty years. That gap requires no judgment to collect. It is the largest structural advantage a retail investor has.

Active management can win, but it has to win after fees. Retail participation in A-shares is high and pricing is less efficient than in the US, so the room for excess return is real. That does not mean you personally capture it. The test is plain: five consecutive years, net of fees, against the same benchmark.

## Do not mistake beta for alpha

In a bull market everyone is a genius. If the account is up thirty percent, ask what the index did over the same period. Only the excess belongs to your judgment.

Leverage multiplies beta and drawdown alike. Money made with leverage is taken back by the same leverage when the market turns. That is arithmetic, not luck.

## Three questions to ask yourself

1. Did you earn this from company profits, or from another investor's loss?
2. If the market closed for five years tomorrow, would you still hold what you hold?
3. How much of your return can you attribute to your own judgment, and how much to the market?

If you cannot answer, you are still gambling.
